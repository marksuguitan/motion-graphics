## Description

A fast, narrow, regular rhythm comes down to one question: is the heart answering a call, or stuck in a loop? Sinus tachycardia is the normal route running faster for a reason you can find. Most SVT is re-entry, a signal circling through or around the AV node, which is also why vagal maneuvers and adenosine can stop it.
