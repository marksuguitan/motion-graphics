The video was first built in an earlier project as a Remotion (code-rendered) video, outside this production guide: one full VO take per script version, key frames rendered as stills, no per-beat SVGs or per-beat VO. Here is how its files map:

- `1-brief/reference/script-v3.md` is the original `script-v3.md`, the latest script, with its storyboard notes and accuracy check. Its narration carries TTS respellings (uh-den-oh-seen, val-sal-vuh, E C G). `2-script/Sinus Tach vs SVT script v3.md` is the same narration in normal spelling, exported for the outside medical review (Nov 2, 2026).
- `1-brief/reference/storyboard v1/` was `SVT vs Sinus Tach Storyboard.zip` (unzipped): 25 key frames (`f/f01.jpg`…`f/f25.jpg`) rendered from the Remotion build, and `voiceover-timed.mp3`, take B of script v3 (2:46.3, with a 1.5 s lead-in). Reference only: the frames are not beat art and the track isn't split into beats.
- `1-brief/reference/remotion v5/` is the Remotion source (`source-remotion-v5.tar.gz`). Reference for a rebuild: Mark's chambers heart and conduction system as path data (`src/data/art.json`), the loop geometry (`src/Loops.tsx`), the ECG generator (`src/ecg.ts`, `src/Strip.tsx`), the palette (`src/theme.ts`), take B word timings (`src/data/words.json`), two vertical shorts (`src/Short.tsx`, with their VO in `public/`), a logo intro and two thumbnails. Left out: `public/vo.mp3` (identical to the storyboard's `voiceover-timed.mp3`) and `public/maps/lit/` (226 PNGs, 68 MB, regenerated by `tools-genlit.js`).
