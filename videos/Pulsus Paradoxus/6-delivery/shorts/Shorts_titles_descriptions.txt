PULSUS PARADOXUS SHORTS: titles and descriptions

Short 1 - What is it.mp4
Title: Why Your Pulse Fades When You Breathe In #shorts
Description:
Breathing in normally drops systolic pressure a little. When the drop is more than 10 mmHg, it's called pulsus paradoxus, and in the right patient it signals an emergency.
Full explainer: https://youtu.be/QkBhI5X1q80

Short 2 - Why tamponade.mp4
Title: How Cardiac Tamponade Causes Pulsus Paradoxus #shorts
Description:
Fluid in a tight pericardial sac pins the right ventricle, so on inspiration it pushes the septum into the left ventricle. Less LV filling, smaller stroke, bigger systolic drop: ventricular interdependence.
Full explainer: https://youtu.be/QkBhI5X1q80
#tamponade #pulsusparadoxus #cardiology #medschool #emergencymedicine

Short 3 - How to measure.mp4
Title: How to Measure Pulsus Paradoxus with a Cuff #shorts
Description:
Manual cuff, normal breathing. Note the pressure where taps appear only on expiration, then where you hear taps on every beat. The difference is the pulsus; over 10 mmHg is pulsus paradoxus.
Full explainer: https://youtu.be/QkBhI5X1q80
