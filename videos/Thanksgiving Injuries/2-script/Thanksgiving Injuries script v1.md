# Five Ways the Feast Ends in the ER (Thanksgiving injuries), script v1

**Audience:** medical students. The video has no narration: everything the viewer learns is on-screen text, shown over simple animation with sound effects.

**Request:** Review this on-screen text for medical accuracy. For each problem, quote the line, say what is wrong, and cite a source.

## Scene 1: Cold open

**1A** · THANKSGIVING / Five ways the feast ends in the ER

## Scene 2: Morning, carving cuts

**2A** · MORNING / Carving cuts / US ERs treat about 1,190 knife injuries a day. [S1]

On-screen text: source note "US average, 1990–2008".

## Scene 3: Afternoon, hot oil

**3A** · AFTERNOON / Hot-oil burns run deeper than they look. [S2, S5]

**3B** · Cool running water, 20 minutes / Within 3 hours. No ice. [S3, S4, S2]

On-screen text: skin layer labels "Epidermis", "Dermis", "Fat"; chip "20 min". The animation shows a hot-oil droplet's damage reaching the deep dermis, then pulling back as cool water runs over it (meant to show a better outcome, not that water reverses a burn).

## Scene 4: Dinner, choking and stuck meat

**4A** · DINNER / 71% / of adult choking deaths: meat, fish or sausage. [S6]

**4B** · Can't speak or cough? / Abdominal thrusts until it clears. [S6]

**4C** · Meat stuck in the esophagus? / 85–90% have a cause: think eosinophilic esophagitis. [S7]

On-screen text: source note "Autopsy series of 200" (4A); labels "Airway", "Esophagus". In 4C the meat lodges in the distal esophagus above a narrowing.

## Scene 5: After dinner, food left out

**5A** · AFTER DINNER / Clostridium perfringens / Its spores survive cooking. [S8]

**5B** · Then: cramps and watery diarrhea / 8 to 16 hours later. [S8]

**5C** · US outbreaks peak in November and December. [S9, S8]

On-screen text: a row of month initials J F M A M J J A S O N D with N and D highlighted.

## Scene 6: Late night, holiday heart

**6A** · LATE NIGHT / Holiday heart / Atrial fibrillation after a binge. [S10, S11]

**6B** · Over 90% revert on their own in 12–24 hours. [S10]

**6C** · Check potassium and magnesium. [S10]

On-screen text: chips "K⁺", "Mg²⁺". An ECG strip shows atrial fibrillation (irregularly irregular, no P waves) turning into sinus rhythm.

## Scene 7: Close

**7A** · Happy Thanksgiving / Medzcool

End screen line: Knives, hot oil, big bites, food left out, one more round.
